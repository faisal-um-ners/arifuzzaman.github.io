## Research perspective

The published parameter maps compare spatial growth in single-layer and two-layer configurations over grating geometry and beam energy.

A two-layer device is advantageous only where its useful growth exceeds that of the single-layer device at the desired operating point. The physical selection rule is therefore based on dispersion and growth, not on structural complexity alone.
