## Research perspective

PASCHEN-1D treats electrode emission through boundary electron fluxes rather than artificial volumetric sources.

The published module includes ion-induced and electron-induced secondary emission, Fowler–Nordheim and Murphy–Good field emission, Richardson–Dushman thermionic emission, prescribed emission, and pulsed photoemission. Multiple mechanisms can be active simultaneously.
