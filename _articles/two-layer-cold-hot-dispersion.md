## Research perspective

For the two-layer structure, the cold-tube problem describes wave propagation without the electron beam and determines the operating point. The hot-tube formulation adds the beam through Maxwell's equations, beam continuity, and electron motion, producing roots $k=k_r+jk_i$.

Together, the two relations separate the questions of where the device operates and how strongly the beam-driven wave grows there.
