## Research perspective

The paper compares spatial growth rate from the hot-tube dispersion relation with starting currents from PIC simulations and finds the same dependence on grating geometry. For fields varying as $E(x)\propto e^{-k_i x}$, negative $k_i$ gives amplification. This makes $-k_i$ a practical design metric for estimating which grating geometries will require less beam current to reach coherent oscillation.
