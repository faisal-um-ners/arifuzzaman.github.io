## Research perspective

For sufficiently wide and thick substrates, the exact theory approaches the familiar diffusive spreading-resistance scaling proportional to $1/a$.

The paper shows that this approximation can fail when the film is ultrathin or the surrounding channel is not sufficiently large. In those regimes, finite geometry and anisotropy must be treated explicitly.
