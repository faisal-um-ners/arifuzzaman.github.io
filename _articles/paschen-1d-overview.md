## Research perspective

PASCHEN-1D is a one-dimensional, time-dependent fluid plasma solver for gas discharge and breakdown. It advances electron and ion continuity equations together with Poisson's equation and couples those plasma dynamics to electrode emission and external circuits.

The implementation is in Python and includes reproducible notebook-based diagnostics.
