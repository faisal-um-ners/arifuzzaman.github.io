## Research perspective

A central result of the two-layer analysis is that the high-spatial-growth region can shift toward lower beam velocities compared with a single-layer grating.

This produces parameter regimes where the two-layer device has stronger growth, and therefore a lower starting-current requirement, at lower beam energy.
