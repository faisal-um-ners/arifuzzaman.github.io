## Research perspective

The published framework starts from

$$
\nabla\cdot(\boldsymbol{\sigma}\nabla\Phi)=0
$$

with anisotropic conductivity tensors in both layers. It includes in-plane and out-of-plane resistivities, finite interfacial resistivity, and finite geometry.

Exact field solutions are derived for both Cartesian and cylindrical contacts, providing a unified treatment that recovers known isotropic limits.
