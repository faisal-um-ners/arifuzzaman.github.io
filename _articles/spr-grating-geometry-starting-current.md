## Research perspective

The published study shows that changing groove height and width, while holding grating period and beam properties fixed, can move the coherent-radiation starting current by orders of magnitude. The cold-tube dispersion determines the operating point, while the hot-tube dispersion gives the complex wavenumber $k=k_r+jk_i$. For the convention used in the paper, negative $k_i$ corresponds to spatial growth. The geometries with the strongest growth are also those with the lowest PIC-derived starting current.
