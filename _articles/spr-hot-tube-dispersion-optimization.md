## Research perspective

The optimization method combines two calculations. Cold-tube dispersion identifies the operating frequency for each grating geometry. Hot-tube dispersion then gives the complex wavenumber at that frequency. Maximizing the magnitude of the negative imaginary part, $-k_i$, identifies geometries with stronger spatial amplification.

The published comparison shows that this trend tracks the starting current obtained from PIC simulation.
