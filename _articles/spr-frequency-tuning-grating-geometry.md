## Research perspective

The cold-tube dispersion curves move when groove height or width changes, so the beam-line intersection changes even for fixed grating period and beam energy. In the parameter range studied, increasing groove height or width generally lowers the evanescent-wave operating frequency.

This highlights a design degree of freedom that is not apparent from the simplest kinematic Smith–Purcell wavelength relation.
