## Research perspective

The two-layer study shows that adding a second periodic layer can increase radiation intensity and reduce starting current for selected parameter sets, but the benefit is not universal.

The controlling quantity is spatial growth rate. Where the two-layer structure has stronger growth than the corresponding single-layer grating, the PIC simulations show lower starting current.
