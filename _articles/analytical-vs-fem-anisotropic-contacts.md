## Research perspective

The exact anisotropic field solutions were compared against finite-element simulations across the reported parameter sweeps and showed excellent agreement.

The framework was also benchmarked against published spreading-resistance measurements on graphite and multilayer MoS$_2$ without fitting parameters. Analytical theory gives direct scaling and speed, while FEM provides flexible visualization and geometry extensions.
